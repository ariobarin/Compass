# Unslop

Start with the author's words. Their cadence, uncertainty, and emphasis are
part of the account. Remove transcription noise and repetition that adds
nothing, but keep a repeated phrase when it carries emphasis or a false start
when it shows a change of mind. Preserve their humor and opinions without
inventing personality.

Organize and clarify so a reader can follow the account. A smoother sentence
is not enough reason to replace one that already works. Read the revision
beside the source and check that the thought and person are still recognizable.
