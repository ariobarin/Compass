# Sources

Read this provenance when auditing or revising Micro Experiment, not during
normal experimentation.

- [What Do Prototypes Prototype?](https://creativetech.mat.ucsb.edu/readings/Prototypes_prototype.pdf): focus a prototype on the important open design question instead of simulating the whole product.
- [Empirical Software Prototyping](https://blog.ploeh.dk/2025/11/17/empirical-software-prototyping/): isolate exploratory code and carry its knowledge, not its implementation, into production.
- [The Best Prototypes Get Thrown Away](https://blog.ravi-mehta.com/p/the-best-prototypes-get-thrown-away): production weight obstructs discovery; a technical prototype can prove a pipeline through a CLI without a UI.
- [An ExperiencedDevs prototype postmortem](https://www.reddit.com/r/ExperiencedDevs/comments/128jkaw): once a successful prototype looks useful, stakeholders readily treat it as a durable product.
