# Source Grounding

Users discover reality. Vendors describe intent.

For behavior, lived experience outranks official posting. Official material
defines contracts, releases, policy, and vendor claims. It does not outrank the
people who bear the product's failures, costs, and constraints.

Field knowledge forms in public. GPT-5.6 had a
[same-day practitioner test](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/9/gpt-5-6/),
[workflow complaints](https://www.reddit.com/r/codex/comments/1utlu6p/is_it_just_me_or_does_56_overcomplicate_tasks/)
within days, and a later
[instrumented investigation](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/35050).
Experience found the problem. Argument sharpened it. Measurement pinned it down.

Research is a hunt for signal. Start where consequences happen: issue trackers,
Reddit, X, practitioner blogs, talks, interviews, and books. Follow promising
trails through artifacts, replies, criticism, and linked work. Dig until the
claim becomes a finding or collapses.

Receipts outrank credentials. Repeated independent experience outranks polish.
Discard launch-copy echoes, generic advice, and certainty without evidence.
Look for exact versions, inputs, outputs, failures, costs, measurements, and
corrections.

Find people with taste. Extract the distinction or words their work earned.
Cite the contribution. Record the date or model when it can expire.
