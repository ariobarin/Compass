# Source Grounding

Source authority and source quality are different questions.

Official material establishes supported features, contracts, policy, and what
its publisher claims. It does not automatically describe lived behavior best.
Users encounter the failures, costs, workarounds, and productive techniques that
only appear through sustained use.

On fast-moving products, that field knowledge starts forming immediately. GPT-5.6
had a [same-day practitioner field report](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/9/gpt-5-6/),
[workflow complaints](https://www.reddit.com/r/codex/comments/1utlu6p/is_it_just_me_or_does_56_overcomplicate_tasks/)
within days, and a later
[instrumented investigation](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/35050).
This progression from observation to argument to measurement often precedes
polished doctrine.

Research from the problem outward. Search issue trackers, Reddit, X,
practitioner blogs, talks, interviews, and books alongside official material.
Follow a promising trail through its artifacts, replies, criticism, and linked
sources. Depth is how an anecdote becomes a finding or a dead end.

Judge sources by what they expose: exact versions, inputs, outputs, failures,
costs, measurements, corrections, and reproducible work. Reputation, popularity,
and provenance are leads. Generic advice, recycled launch copy, and unsupported
certainty are noise regardless of where they appear.

Taste is the judgment to recognize which distinctions matter. Look for writers
who repeatedly leave receipts, calibrate uncertainty, and revise their views.
Extract the principle or wording their work earned, cite it beside its
contribution, and record the date or model when the idea can expire.
